# First walking bridge — v0.2

The terrarium combines a **partial observed MANC graph**, an **assumed firing-rate model**, and a **designed muscle recruitment / coordination bridge**. It is not a reconstructed whole fly, a measured motor program, or a demonstration that the selected circuit alone explains walking. Most recruitment still comes from the experimental bridge. Turning that bridge off is deliberately available in the interface.

## Observational substrate

The graph contains 532 cells: two DNg100 descending neurons, 18 cells of the candidate six-leg CPG motifs, 392 annotated leg motor neurons, and 120 additional intrinsic neurons. There are 10,279 retained directed edges. Counts are unchanged integers; transmitter signs are separate inferred fields. No synapse is created by training, reward or weight optimization.

Data were extracted from [Pugliese et al.'s public research repository](https://github.com/smpuglie/Pugliese_cpg_2025) at `faee4b06869855ae0164cbf217fb6ec28ef3521b`, using `wTable_20260522_allSynapses.feather` and `W_20260522_allSynapses.npz`. The public JSON records both file hashes. Matrix rows are presynaptic, columns postsynaptic. Absolute values are anatomical counts; signs in the upstream matrix encode transmitter predictions, not measured conductance. The [MANC provider](https://www.janelia.org/project-team/flyem/manc-connectome) licenses its data under CC-BY. Credit HHMI Janelia FlyEM, Cambridge Connectomics, Google Research, and the annotating research teams. No upstream simulation software is redistributed.

Selection is anatomical: for each leg, rank intrinsic neurons by total absolute input from the 20 core/descending cells multiplied by total absolute output to that leg's motor neurons, retain the top 20, then retain all nonzero edges within the resulting union. This boundary is a modelling choice and excludes most of the VNC. It is not the full paper's pruning experiment, and omitted inputs can radically change dynamics. No behavioral outcome was used to select these additional cells.

The 20260522 snapshot does not by itself establish an exact neuPrint materialization identifier. We pin the actual files rather than silently invent a release. The source anatomy and MANC come from different specimens. FANC, MaleCNS and hemibrain remain separate references, not merged observed graphs.

## Neural model

`tau dr_i/dt = max(0, r_cap tanh[a_i (I_i + b sum_j sign_j count_ji r_j - theta_i) / r_cap]) - r_i`

Independent RK4 implementation in `src/circuit.ts`. Mean parameters follow the source model: tau 20 ms, cap 200 model Hz, baseline gain 1, threshold 7.5, count-to-input gain 0.03. Gain is divided by relative cell size and threshold multiplied by it. The size reference is 666,270,409, the median of the complete 4,604-cell MANC T1 reference table, **not** the selected subset. Transferring that reference to other legs is an assumption. No heterogeneity is sampled. DNg100 receives a tonic 250 model-unit input. These are neither measured currents nor identified receptor kinetics. Glutamate is assumed inhibitory in central connections; this does not imply inhibitory glutamatergic NMJs.

The pure motif assay uses IDs 10093, 10707, 11751, 13905 and edges of at least five synapses between them. This recovers Fig. 3's seven nonzero edges. The main 532-cell simulation retains weaker edges too. Its behavior need not match the isolated motif or the full paper network.

## Explicit bridge hypotheses

| ID             | Runtime mechanism                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Status                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| H-coordination | Detect LF E2 peaks; two neural cycles per stride; alternate LF/LH/RM with RF/RH/LM; 60% stance                                                                                                                     | Designed shared timing, not an observed all-leg projection from LF E2. No neural peaks means no continuing stride clock.                                                     |
| H-posture      | Linearize each leg's actual hinge Jacobian at the neutral stance; convert a 0.4 mm stroke / 0.3 mm lift into joint targets; proportional joint feedback 50 and derivative feedback 0.12 recruit antagonist muscles | Ideal proprioception and a hand-specified movement template. This fills missing premotor/reflex/NMJ circuitry and is the principal source of recruitment. No learned policy. |
| H-MN           | Mean model motor-pool rate adds a small excitation contribution; network-only mode uses mean rate / 40                                                                                                             | Pooling and excitation gains are assumptions; individual NMJs and motor-unit force differences unresolved.                                                                   |
| H-muscle       | 18 principal joint DOF, 36 antagonistic groups, constant 0.05 mm moment arms, native Hill activation and force-length/velocity dynamics                                                                            | Moment arms are signed tendon-excursion approximations, not reconstructed attachment paths. Anatomical attachment fidelity remains available in the unchanged foreleg bench. |
| H-odor         | Bilateral exponentially decaying scalar fields; food/repellent difference alters left/right stride size                                                                                                            | Behavioral approximation, not reconstructed olfactory circuitry.                                                                                                             |
| H-energy       | Food-zone occupancy increments an energy proxy; a reward is exposed for environment experiments                                                                                                                    | No ingestion, metabolism, organ physiology or learning implementation.                                                                                                       |

New muscle parameters: maximum isometric group force 180 µN; activation/deactivation 8/25 ms; normalized muscle-length range 0.5–1.5 with signed tendon excursion endpoints −0.16/+0.16 mm. These are hand-chosen experimental parameters. Joint armature is 0.000002 g·mm², damping 0.02 g·mm²/s; joint limits, initial stance and reduced joint axes are hypotheses. They were adjusted during software debugging to obtain finite, supported mechanics, not fitted to fly recordings. No biological uncertainty interval is claimed from those debugging runs.

## Body and physical scope

`scripts/derive-walker.py` generates a separate `walking.xml` from preserved FlyMimic geometry and segment offsets. `fly.xml` and its source hashes remain untouched. Relative masses and inertias are retained but scaled to an **assumed** 1 mg animal (plus negligible socket masses), using grams, millimetres and seconds so force units are µN. This convention does not validate the original source model's force scale. Tarsi are rigid segments; contact uses foot spheres, a thorax ellipsoid, a floor and arena walls. Mesh self-collision is disabled. Substrate friction and contact softness are modelling assumptions.

Only muscle controls act on the body during stepping. No root translation, pose animation, teleportation or applied root force is used. Reset sets the declared initial pose. The joint target template is a controller hypothesis, not a geometric animation of the fly. Wings, halteres, sensory organs, gut, circulation, respiration and reproduction are not functional simulations. Banana scenery has illustrative scale and no collision; the named food and repellent patches supply functional fields. Water is a visible environment object without drinking physiology.

## Reproduction and validation

Node 24: `npm ci`, `npm run verify`. `node scripts/assay-walking.mjs` writes a bounded, source-hashed assay artifact. Tests cover original source hashes, antagonist bench responses, minimal neural motif lesions, neural timestep convergence, movement with muscles, all-E1 silencing, passive collapse, and preservation of locomotion with half the physical timestep. These are software/causal controls, not biological validation.

Data regeneration requires Python, numpy, pandas and pyarrow; the full matrix briefly needs roughly 4.5 GB of memory. Clone the cited repository into ignored `work/Pugliese_cpg_2025`, check out the exact commit, then run `python scripts/extract-network-source.py` and `python scripts/extract-manc.py`. Derive the body with standard-library Python: `python scripts/derive-walker.py`. `scripts/walking-stance.json` records the neutral pose obtained by inverse kinematics to declared foot positions; it is not a measured fly pose or a learned controller.

## Supplied navigation reference

The [September 14 thread](https://x.com/BrainsAndTennis/status/2099561735648890898) leads to [Wang and Christie's analysis](https://pwang724.github.io/fly-circuit-exploration/findings/01-synaptic-store.html) and [repository](https://github.com/pwang724/fly-circuit-exploration). We read the full first finding and its limitations. It proposes synaptic home-vector storage in hΔA/H/I/G; it also explicitly acknowledges activity-based storage remains possible, plasticity has not been recorded at these joins, and a reset or weakening rule is required to avoid saturation. Its table does **not** supply uniform dopamine gating to hΔG or broad OA-VPM3 reset input to hΔI. These details constrain how the social summary should be interpreted.

This is a useful hypothesis lane, not permission to fill unmeasured plasticity with a trained navigation policy. Before implementation, audit source edges and independent physiology, test activity-storage alternatives, and predict interventions that distinguish the mechanisms. Current runtime has no hΔ plasticity or home-vector memory.

Next: replace H-posture on one foreleg with the identified CxHP8 reflex, resolve a motor unit to its fibres/NMJ, and replace H-coordination with observed inter-leg pathways. Expand coverage only after those interventions pass against fly data.
